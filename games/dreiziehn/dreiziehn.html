How to play
  • Deck: one 60-card deck per player: 52 cards plus two jokers per suit. In multiplayer every player gets their own deck.
  • Cards: numbered 1–13; the card colour runs from blue (1) to red (13).
  • Start: shuffle the deck, deal yourself 5 hand cards, and leave the rest face down in the stock.
  • Turn: play at least one card, then end your turn. Ending the turn draws back up to 5 from the stock; in multiplayer the turn then passes to the next player.
  • Middle: four shared middle piles. Each starts with a 1 and counts up to a 13; suit doesn't matter. A joker is wild. A completed 13 pile is set aside and its slot starts again at 1. Everyone sees the same middle cards.
  • Your piles: four free storage piles. Any card, any order. Click a pile to take its top card into your hand (when your hand has room), or play a selected hand card onto it. They must be empty to win.
  • Win: empty the stock and all four of your piles. Cards left in your hand are fine. In multiplayer the first player to do this wins.

Tap a card in your hand, then tap a middle pile or one of your piles. Tap the selected card again to put it back.
